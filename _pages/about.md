<div class="about-text" markdown="1">

I am a PhD student at the University of Alberta, advised by [Marlos C. Machado](https://webdocs.cs.ualberta.ca/~machado/). Previously, I completed my M.Sc. in Computer Science at the University of Massachusetts Amherst, working with [Philip S. Thomas](https://people.cs.umass.edu/~pthomas/) and [Scott Niekum](https://people.cs.umass.edu/~sniekum/). I received my B.Sc. in Computer Science at the Chinese University of Hong Kong.

My research focuses on representation learning in reinforcement learning. Specifically, I study incorporating additional information (e.g., reward) into temporal predictive representations (e.g., the successor representation, the Laplacian representation) and its potential benefits in various applications (e.g., option discovery, zero-shot transfer).
